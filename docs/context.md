# BetOff — Project Context

## Problem Statement

Gambling addiction affects millions of people worldwide. Current options are binary: keep gambling or go cold turkey. Cold turkey has a high relapse rate because it leaves the underlying urge unaddressed. Boring substitutes fail too — if the alternative isn't as compelling as the real thing, the user goes back. BetOff works because it **meets the user at their current stimulus level**, then guides them down gradually, like tapering a medication instead of stopping abruptly.

## Product Philosophy

BetOff is a harm-reduction tool, not an abstinence enforcer. We do not shame users for wanting to gamble. We compete with real platforms on excitement — then, slowly, we reduce every stimulus parameter over time:

- **Progressive stimulus taper** — we start where the user is (full sounds, animations, fast results, vibrant colors), then reduce each parameter on a transparent schedule.
- **Recovery stimulus replaces gambling stimulus** — as game excitement fades, achievements, milestones, and community recognition grow. The user always has something rewarding to engage with.
- **Virtual currency only** — there is nothing to lose financially, at any stimulus level.
- **Mandatory reflection** — every session ends with a prompt that reconnects the user to their recovery intent, from day one.

## Target Persona

**The Self-Aware Addict**

- Knows they have a gambling problem and genuinely wants to stop.
- Still experiences strong urges, especially in idle moments or after stress.
- Has tried to quit before but relapsed — either because the urge was too strong or because the substitute was too boring.
- Needs a "pressure valve" that feels as good as the real thing, at first, and helps them wean off over time.
- Is not currently in crisis but benefits from low-friction daily support.

BetOff is not a crisis intervention tool. Users in acute crisis should be directed to professional help.

## Core Principles

These constraints are non-negotiable. Every feature decision must be compatible with all five:

1. **No real money, ever.** No deposits, no withdrawals, no in-app purchases. The virtual balance exists only within BetOff.
2. **Graduated delay.** Result speed starts near-instant (30 seconds) and increases over time, reaching 24 hours at the lowest stimulus level.
3. **Stimulus taper.** Sounds, animations, and color intensity reduce progressively across 5 levels. The platform is never more stimulating than the user's current level. Users always know their level and can advance voluntarily — never forced back.
4. **Recovery stimulus replaces gambling stimulus.** Achievements, streaks, and community recognition grow as game excitement decreases, so the user always has a rewarding reason to return.
5. **Mandatory reflection.** Every session — defined as a period of active play — ends with a short journaling prompt before the user can leave the games section. This is not skippable, at any stimulus level.

## The Stimulus Taper at a Glance

| Level | Name | When | Experience |
|-------|------|------|-----------|
| 5 | Rush | Days 0–7 | Full sounds, animations, confetti, vibrant colors, 30s results |
| 4 | High | Days 8–21 | 60% volume, slower animations, warm tones, 5 min results |
| 3 | Medium | Days 22–60 | 25% volume, fade transitions only, muted colors, 30 min results |
| 2 | Low | Days 61–120 | Silent, no animations, calm palette, 4h results |
| 1 | Minimal | Day 121+ | Silent, no animations, full BetOff calm palette, 24h results |

## What BetOff Is Not

- Not a therapy platform or a replacement for professional mental health support.
- Not a social gambling site. The community forum is for peer support, not competitive play.
- Not a real-money platform in any jurisdiction.
- Not responsible for clinical outcomes. Users should consult a professional for addiction treatment.

## Monetization

BetOff is a non-profit project. It is sustained by voluntary donations and grants. There are no premium tiers, no paywalls, and no advertising. Donation links are visible but never intrusive.

## Success Metrics (Product Health)

- Reduction in reported real-gambling sessions (self-reported in reflection prompts).
- Session reflection completion rate (target: >80%).
- Taper progression rate: percentage of users who reach Level 3 or below.
- Return rate: users coming back to BetOff instead of real platforms.
- Forum engagement: posts and replies as a signal of community health.
